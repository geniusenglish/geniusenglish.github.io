Teacher Guide

THE GO-KART · AR 0.3

1. Quick Summary

Biff and Chip learn to share a go-kart and take turns riding it.

2. Key Vocabulary

go-kart고카트
share함께 쓰다, 나누다
take turns차례를 지키다
ride타다

3. Book Talk Questions & Answers

Q1. What are Biff and Chip doing?
A. They are riding a go-kart.
Q2. How do they share the go-kart?
A. They take turns riding it.
Q3. What was your favorite part? Why?
A. Sample: My favorite part was when they rode the go-kart because it looked fun.
Q4. Why did you like it?
A. Sample: I liked it because Biff and Chip had fun together.

4. 3-Sentence Summary Writing

학생이 책의 내용을 Beginning → Middle → Ending 순서로 3문장으로 정리하도록 지도합니다.

1.Biff and Chip have a go-kart.

2.They learn to share it and take turns riding.

3.They have fun riding the go-kart together.
